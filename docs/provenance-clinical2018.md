# Clinical 2018 provenance issue

## What we know
The Week 1 brief describes approximately 10,000 supplied clinical-2018 spectra and notes that 25 x 400 is arithmetically compatible with that count.

## What we do not know
No verified isolate IDs, patient IDs, acquisition-order metadata, or repository source documentation establishes whether the intended source is 25/10,000 or 30/12,000.

## Possible explanations
Subset, upstream exclusion, alternate release/split, prior filtering, or a descriptive-count error. These are hypotheses only.

## Evidence required
Original citation/repository, version, split code, acquisition metadata, and explicit isolate mapping.

## Impact
Until identity is established, spectra cannot safely support isolate-level or patient-level independence claims.

## Required resolution
Trace the delivered array to a source version, reconcile counts, and attach an immutable provenance-backed isolate mapping before isolate-level validation.
