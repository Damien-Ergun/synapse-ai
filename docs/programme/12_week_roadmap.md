# Raman Bacteria Prototype 0, controlled 12-week roadmap reference

The authoritative source is `12-week-Roadmap.txt` with the SHA-256 recorded above. This controlled repository reference adopts that roadmap and preserves its Week 1 execution requirements.

## Week 1 objective

Create the project foundation and define what is being built, what is not being built, and which future clinical decision the platform could eventually support.

## Week 1 workstreams

- Technical and scientific: repository, Python 3.11 and uv environment, architecture, checks, and metric contract.
- Product and clinical: Target Product Profile v0, open questions, testable future hypothesis, and evidence ladder.
- Business and investment: company thesis, stakeholder map, problem-first interview script, five interview requests, and investment hypotheses.
- Governance, regulation and IP: claims, risks, decisions, data governance, dataset rights, dependency licences, and code ownership.

## Week 1 gate

The repository installs and tests from scratch; prototype and non-prototype claims are explicit; the future use case is testable; major technical, clinical, and commercial risks are visible; and at least five stakeholder interviews have been requested.

## Week 2 objective

Build a trustworthy dataset layer and begin validating the proposed clinical problem. Dataset array access remains prohibited until DEC-021 is superseded by authoritative rights evidence.

## Programme boundary

Prototype 0 is not a medical diagnostic product. Later weeks own data validation, preprocessing, models, locked testing, clinical-split evaluation, calibration and rejection, reporting, application development, investment materials, and independent verification.
