# Evidence ladder

| Level | Name | Required evidence | Permitted claim | Prohibited claim | Exit gate |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Reliable software foundation | Locked environment, tests, governance, reproducibility | A research pipeline foundation exists | Any model, clinical, or diagnostic performance claim | Week 1 gate |
| 1 | Public bacteria benchmark | Validated data, controlled preprocessing, locked evaluation | Performance on the named public bacterial benchmark | Transfer to blood, serum, plasma, or clinical care | Weeks 2 to 8 gates |
| 2 | Acquisition and instrument robustness | Controlled non-clinical acquisition, repeatability, protocol and instrument evidence | Acquisition feasibility under the tested setup | Disease classification or clinical utility | Instrument feasibility gate |
| 3 | Retrospective serum or plasma feasibility | Controlled retrospective samples, labels, protocol, statistics | Feasibility for the specified study population and endpoint | Prospective clinical effectiveness or regulatory readiness | Retrospective study gate |
| 4 | Prospective silent clinical study | Prospective protocol, no impact on care, predeclared analysis | Prospective silent-study performance | Treatment or routine-care recommendation | Prospective study gate |
| 5 | Regulated clinical product | Analytical, clinical, usability, safety, QMS, regulatory evidence | Claims within the authorized intended purpose | Claims outside authorization | Regulatory authorization and lifecycle controls |

Public bacteria evidence cannot skip Levels 2 to 4. Every external claim must identify the level and exact evidence record.
