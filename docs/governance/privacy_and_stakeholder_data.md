# Privacy and stakeholder data

## Consent and collection

Before an interview, state the research purpose, expected 30-minute duration, note-taking method, voluntary nature, and whether recording is requested. Recording is off by default and requires explicit consent. Do not collect identifiable patient information, clinical records, trade secrets, or confidential institutional material.

## Public records

The repository may contain only anonymous outreach IDs, role category, organisation type, date, channel, status, evidence question, and private evidence ID. It must not contain names, personal email addresses, phone numbers, message bodies, recordings, or private notes.

## Private records

A local crosswalk may map outreach IDs to contacts. It remains outside Git, access-controlled, and is deleted after 12 months of inactivity unless an active collaboration requires retention. Interview records use anonymous IDs and paraphrase evidence unless explicit quotation permission is recorded.

## Status vocabulary

`TARGET_IDENTIFIED`, `DRAFT_READY`, `REQUEST_SENT`, `REPLIED`, `SCHEDULED`, `COMPLETED`, `DECLINED`, and `NO_RESPONSE` are the only outreach statuses.

## Incident response

If private information is staged or committed, stop publication, remove the material without exposing it further, rotate affected credentials where applicable, document the incident, and seek GitHub history-removal support if needed. Force pushing is not an unreviewed default response.
