## Rights findings

The publication and author repository establish public availability for replication. The repository software licence does not automatically prove that externally hosted spectrum arrays are licensed under the same terms. No separate authoritative dataset licence, redistribution permission, derivative-output licence, or commercial-use permission has been retained in the project evidence set.

## Authorization decision

1. an authoritative dataset-specific licence covering the arrays and the planned use; or

Publication pages, repository metadata, and licence files may be inspected without downloading arrays.
