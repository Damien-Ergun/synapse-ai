# Company thesis

## Controlled one-sentence thesis

I am developing a Raman AI platform intended to extract clinically useful information from biological samples, beginning with a reproducible bacterial-spectrum classification engine and progressing, only if evidence supports it, toward a controlled serum or plasma feasibility study.

## Current fact versus future hypothesis

The current fact is that a software and governance foundation is being built. The bacterial benchmark, clinically useful output, serum or plasma feasibility, customer value, and defensibility are hypotheses until their assigned evidence gates pass.
