# Customer-discovery interview script

## Consent opening

Thank the participant, explain that this is a 30-minute research conversation about current laboratory or clinical workflows, confirm voluntary participation and note-taking consent, state that recording is off unless separately approved, and ask the participant not to share patient-identifiable or confidential institutional information.

## Current workflow

1. Walk me through the current pathway from sample collection to the decision you need.
2. Which people, systems, instruments, and hand-offs are involved?
3. Where do delays, repeats, uncertainty, or unusable samples occur?
4. Which decisions are delayed or made with incomplete information?

## Consequences and requirements

5. What happens when the result is late, wrong, or unavailable?
6. Which false-negative, false-positive, and uncertain outcomes are least tolerable?
7. What sample preparation, instrument time, staff time, and training are acceptable?
8. Which quality, confidence, alternative-class, and rejection information would be required?

## Alternatives and adoption

9. Which current alternatives are used, and why?
10. Who operates, interprets, approves, buys, and maintains a new system?
11. What evidence would be required before a silent research pilot?
12. What would make this approach not worth testing?

## Close

Summarise the main points, ask what was misunderstood, request permission for follow-up, and record supported and contradicted hypotheses without pitching diagnostic readiness.
