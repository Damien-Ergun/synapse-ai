# Outreach templates

All templates request a 30-minute problem-discovery conversation, state that the project is research-only, and avoid performance or diagnostic claims.

## Clinical microbiology or laboratory professional

Hello, I am building the research foundation for a Raman spectrum analysis project and am trying to understand current microbiology laboratory workflows before defining a product. Would you be available for a 30-minute conversation about bottlenecks, sample and instrument constraints, required result information, and what evidence would be needed before testing a research prototype? I will not request patient-identifiable or confidential institutional information.

## Clinician

Hello, I am studying where delays or uncertainty in infectious-disease pathways create difficult decisions. I am not evaluating a diagnostic product yet. Would you be available for a 30-minute problem-discovery conversation about current decisions, turnaround needs, error consequences, and acceptable handling of uncertain results?

## Raman spectroscopy specialist

Hello, I am establishing a reproducible bacterial Raman analysis benchmark and a future non-diagnostic acquisition feasibility plan. Would you be available for a 30-minute technical discussion about acquisition controls, artefacts, repeatability, instrument access, and what protocol detail a laboratory would need to review?

## Innovation office or instrument partner

Hello, I am defining an early research programme for Raman analysis of biological samples. Would you be available for a 30-minute discussion about research access, evaluation pathways, integration constraints, and the evidence required before considering collaboration? The current work makes no clinical performance claim.

## Regulatory, funding, or investment stakeholder

Hello, I am building a research-use Raman software and evidence programme and want to test its assumptions before presenting an investment case. Would you be available for a 30-minute conversation about evidence thresholds, intended-purpose risks, milestones, adoption barriers, and what would make the programme not fundable or not reviewable?
